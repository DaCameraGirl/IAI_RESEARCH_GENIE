 Description
This Study is directed to previously unidentified prior art references, including patent and non-patent literature.

Two known ligands for PD-1, PD-L1 (B7-H1) and PD-L2 (B7-DC), are expressed in human cancers arising in various tissues. An antibody that blocks binding of the human PD-L1 and PD-L2 (receptors located on a tumor cell) to the human PD-1 (receptors located on a T-cell) can block this interaction and allow the T-cells to attack the cancer cell.

Specifically, we are interested in the use of pembrolizumab (an anti-PD-1 monoclonal antibody comprising a heavy chain and a light chain, wherein the heavy and light chains comprise SEQ ID NO:21 and SEQ ID NO:22; see Study Patents; it may also be known as MK-3475, SCH 900475 or lambrolizumab) in combination with axitinib (a VEGF receptors inhibitor: N-methyl-2-[3-((E)-2-pyridin-2-yl-vinyl)-1H-indazol-6-ylsulfanyl]-benzamide as shown below; marketed under the brand name Inlyta), to treat cancer.

Specifically, we want to see axitinib administered at one of the following doses:

 5 mg twice daily (BID)
 3 mg BID (i.e., twice daily) 
in combination with Pembrolizumab administered at of 1 mg/kg every three weeks (i.e., Q3W), 2 mg/kg Q3W or 200 mg Q3W.

This combination therapy can be used to treat several different types of cancer, including:  bladder cancer, breast cancer, clear cell kidney cancer, head/neck squamous cell carcinoma, lung squamous cell carcinoma, malignant melanoma, non-small-cell lung cancer (NSCLC), ovarian cancer, pancreatic cancer, prostate cancer, renal cell cancer, small-cell lung cancer (SCLC), triple negative breast cancer, acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL), acute myeloid leukemia (AML), chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL), chronic myeloid leukemia (CML), diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL), follicular lymphoma, Hodgkin’s lymphoma (HL), mantle cell lymphoma (MCL), multiple myeloma (MM), myelodysplastic syndrome (MDS), non-Hodgkin’s lymphoma (NHL), or small lymphocytic lymphoma (SLL).

Axitinib blocks proteins that help cancer cells grow and form new blood vessels, thereby limiting tumor growth.  Specifically, it is a TKI that selectively blocks vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) receptors.  Because VEGF signals drive angiogenesis, which supports tumor growth and metastasis, inhibiting this pathway can suppress tumor growth.  Axitinib inhibition of VEGF signaling can also shift the tumor microenvironment from immunosuppressive to immune-stimulatory, which may enhance the antitumor effects of anti-PD-1 antibodies such as pembrolizumab.

A method of treating cancer comprising:

1.1 
administering pembrolizumab (anti-PD-1 monoclonal antibody which comprises a heavy chain and a light chain, wherein the heavy and light chains comprise SEQ ID NO:21 and SEQ ID NO:22; see Study Patents) and axitinib (an VEGF receptors inhibitor: N-methyl-2-[3-((E)-2-pyridin-2-yl-vinyl)-1H-indazol-6-ylsulfanyl]-benzamide; marketed under the brand name Inlyta);

1.2 
wherein the cancer is bladder cancer, breast cancer, clear cell kidney cancer, head/neck squamous cell carcinoma, lung squamous cell carcinoma, malignant melanoma, non-small-cell lung cancer, ovarian cancer, pancreatic cancer, prostate cancer, renal cell cancer, small-cell lung cancer, triple negative breast cancer, acute lymphoblastic leukemia, acute myeloid leukemia, chronic lymphocytic leukemia, chronic myeloid leukemia, diffuse large B-cell lymphoma, follicular lymphoma, Hodgkin's lymphoma, mantle cell lymphoma, multiple myeloma, myelodysplastic syndrome, non-Hodgkin's lymphoma, or small lymphocytic lymphoma.

1.3 
wherein the renal cell carcinoma is an advanced renal cell carcinoma that is predominantly clear-cell subtype with primary tumor resected;

1.4 
wherein axitinib is administered at a starting dose of 5 mg twice daily (i.e., “BID” or “B.I.D.”)

1.5 
wherein if the individual tolerates axitinib with no drug-related grade >2 adverse events for two consecutive weeks, the dose of Axitinib is then increased to a maximum dose of 10 mg BID (i.e., twice daily);

1.6 
wherein if the dose combination is not tolerated, the dose of axitinib is then reduced to a minimum dose of 3 mg BID (i.e., twice daily);

1.7 
wherein pembrolizumab is administered at a dose of 1 mg/kg every three weeks (i.e., “Q3W”), 2 mg/kg Q3W, or 200 mg Q3W.
