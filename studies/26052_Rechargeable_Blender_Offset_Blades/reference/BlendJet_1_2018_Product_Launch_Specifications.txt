PRODUCT LAUNCH SPECIFICATIONS & FEATURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. WORLD'S MOST POWERFUL PORTABLE BLENDER
   - Cordless, USB-rechargeable handheld portable blender designed for anywhere use.
   - Ideal for gym, travel, outdoors, work, or home.

2. PATENTED TURBOJET TECHNOLOGY & OFFSET BLADES
   - Stainless steel blades are offset from the center of the base, creating a tornado effect that blasts ingredients into the back of the jar 275 times per second.
   - Offset blade arrangement prevents jamming, eliminates air pockets, and delivers superior blending efficiency compared to traditional centered blades.

3. PHYSICAL & OPERATIONAL SPECIFICATIONS
   - Upright vertical pitcher arrangement mounted directly on motor base.
   - 6-Point Stainless Steel Blade assembly.
   - 4000 mAh Rechargeable Battery (15+ blends per charge).
   - USB-C Water-Resistant Rechargeable Port.
   - Compact Dimensions: 9" x 3" (230mm x 76mm).
   - Capacity: 16 oz (475 mL).

REQUIRMENTS & CLAIMS COVERAGE:
- Requirement 1.1: Rotating 6-blade stainless steel assembly with defined rotational axis.
- Requirement 1.2: Blade rotational axis offset laterally from container central longitudinal axis.
- Requirement 1.3: ~10% blade diameter offset ratio across container base.
- PREF-1: Cordless, USB-rechargeable handheld portable blender.
- PREF-2: Upright vertical arrangement of container, blade, and base.
- PREF-3: Tornado/vortex rationale for preventing food jamming and air pockets.
